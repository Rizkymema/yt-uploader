Privacy Policy

yt-uploader · Last updated: October 28, 2026

yt-uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop application, developed by Rizky Mema, that lets a user upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API v3. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests only this permission (OAuth scope):

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – to upload videos to your YouTube channel.

The app uses this permission only to upload the video files you choose, together with the title, description, tags, category and privacy setting you enter. It does not read, edit or delete your existing videos, comments, subscribers or other YouTube or Google account data.

2. How data is stored

  • The app runs entirely on your own computer. There is no developer server; your videos and data are sent directly from your computer to Google/YouTube.
  • After you sign in, an OAuth access/refresh token is saved locally in a file named token.json on your computer so you don't have to sign in every time. You can delete this file at any time.
  • The developer does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data, videos, or Google account information.

3. Sharing with third parties

Data is shared only with Google/YouTube, as needed to upload your video. No data is shared with advertisers or any other third party.

4. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

yt-uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also delete the local token.json file.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
