yt-uploader

A personal desktop video uploader for YouTube

yt-uploader is a small desktop application used to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channel. It runs on the user's computer and uses the official YouTube Data API v3.

What it does

  • Uploads a video file you choose to your YouTube channel.
  • Sets the title, description, tags, category and privacy status (private, unlisted or public).
  • Asks only for the youtube.upload permission – it cannot read or delete your existing videos.

Your data

Videos go directly from your computer to YouTube. The developer does not run a server for this app and does not collect your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
